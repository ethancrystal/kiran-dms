**Task:** frontend structure plan (docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-05-frontend-structure.md); records MJ's answers to its open questions
**What / why:** MJ reviewed the draft frontend structure plan in chat on 2026-11-05 and approved its direction with corrections. Recorded as MJ gave them:
- The foundation F1 to F8 is approved. Generated types must reflect the real Supabase schema and migrations. The RPC client is typed and maps errors consistently and never bypasses database authorisation. One design system serves the Office, Rep and Salon applications. Navigation is permission-aware and hiding an item is not a security control. Invoice and receipt views use authoritative database records, with no locally calculated financial figures. Test helpers come before the complex workflows. The offline outbox uses stable idempotency keys and reconciles against server results.
- The full offline outbox is not a prerequisite for every screen. Office and Salon screens are online first. Rep offline capability must be complete before the field pilot.
- Slices S-A to S-F are approved as dependency-managed work packages, not a linear sequence. S-A verifies and completes the existing inventory work. S-B reconciles and completes P1-07 (PR 46 needs independent verification; the order redesign follows D-30). S-C builds on the existing territory, salon, visit and due-list work. S-D establishes the approval boundaries early. S-E builds from verified reporting functions with no browser-side financial or stock arithmetic. S-F is a field-pilot requirement, not a blocker for Office and Salon work.
- Delivery approval: a rep may submit delivery evidence and quantities and cannot mark an order officially delivered. Only the manager or owner approves, enforced in PostgreSQL and the RPC. Approval atomically finalises the delivery, posts the stock movements, issues the invoice and preserves the commission attribution. A rep never approves their own submission. Unrelated approval-lifecycle questions do not block this workflow.
- The Office sidebar is grouped by business workflow, collapsible, highlights the current route, is permission-aware for owner and manager, scales with new modules, and does not list unfinished modules as working items. Groups: Overview; Customers and Salons; Orders and Deliveries; Inventory and Stock; Money and Collections; Field Team; Controls and Approvals; Reports and Analytics; Administration and Settings.
- The interface is English only for the current release (D-42). Roman Urdu stays accepted in free-text fields: salon notes, rep visit notes, New Order Details cards, customer-provided descriptions, internal notes and reasons. It is stored as entered, with Unicode and UTF-8 preserved.
- System-generated invoice and receipt wording is English only for the current release, including the sentence that the system receipt is the only valid proof of payment. Doc 08 is updated. The ability to store Urdu and Roman Urdu customer data is unchanged.
- The Office application has a dedicated Rep-detail page: an operational oversight page, not an unrestricted editing page, following the existing permission matrix, with historical records immutable and corrections through authorised audited workflows.
- Every screen documents route, purpose, permitted roles, RPCs, data dependencies, states and acceptance criteria. Each exists, planned or missing classification is checked against the real routes and components. Each implemented screen has at least one meaningful stack or browser test of UI-to-backend behaviour and role restrictions.
- The plan is an architectural roadmap under the existing phase plan and decision register, not a competing plan. Decisions already recorded are not reopened. The commission calculation basis is still subject to a separate explicit policy decision: the frontend hard-codes no invoice-based commission formula and does not present commission payouts as an approved feature.
