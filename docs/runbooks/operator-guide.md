# How to run the business with Kiran DMS

For the manager and the owner. No technical knowledge is needed. The system does the adding up for you: every number you see is worked out from what has been recorded, at the moment you open the page. You never need to add anything up by hand.

## Signing in

1. Open the office address on your computer or phone.
2. Type your email and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
3. You land on **Overview**. This is your home page.

## Your 5-minute routine every morning

Open **Overview** and go down the page from top to bottom.

1. **The four boxes at the top.** They tell you, in numbers:
   - how many orders are waiting to be delivered,
   - how many deliveries are waiting for your approval,
   - how many orders were placed this week,
   - how many were delivered this week.
2. **Needs your attention.** Deal with each line, one by one. Each line has a link that takes you to the right page.
   - *Deliveries waiting for your approval*: a rep has handed goods to a salon. Open **Delivery approvals**, check the quantities, and approve or reject. Nothing is invoiced until you approve.
   - *Order requests waiting*: a rep or a salon has asked for goods. Open **Orders** and turn each request into an order, or reject it with a short reason.
   - *New salons waiting for approval*: a rep has proposed a new salon. Open **Salon approvals** and approve or reject it.
   - *Orders on hold*: the line tells you why (for example, the salon has reached its credit limit). Decide whether to release the order or cancel it.
   - *Rep days left open*: a rep did not check out, or worked past midnight, and cannot check in today. Go to **Rep days left open** lower on the Overview, click **Close this day**, type why, and confirm. The rep can then check in.
   - *Requests waiting for a decision*: a discount or a stock adjustment. Open **Approvals**.
   - *Approved stock adjustments not posted*: open **Stock** and post them.
   - *Goods from a refused delivery not settled*: open **Delivery approvals** and put them back in the bag or the warehouse, or record them as missing.
   - *Stock issues disputed*: the rep counted something different. Open **Stock** and check it.
   - *Phones waiting for approval*: open **Devices**.
   - *Cash*: a rep at the cash limit, a count waiting for the rep's answer, or a disputed count. Open **Cash handover**.
3. **Waiting to be delivered.** The orders that still need to reach the salon, the ones waiting longest at the top. The last column tells you the next step:
   - "The rep delivers it": nothing for you to do yet.
   - "Rep delivered it: approve": go to **Delivery approvals**.
   - "Office delivers": the office must send it from the warehouse.
4. **Delivered.** The latest deliveries, each with its invoice number and amount. Use it to answer a salon that asks "did my order arrive?".

If the list says "Nothing is waiting for you", you are done.

## What each page shows

| Page | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Overview | Your home page: orders placed, waiting, delivered, and what needs your attention. |
| Orders | Make an order, turn a request into an order, and see every order. |
| Delivery approvals | Approve or reject what reps say they delivered. |
| Salons, Salon approvals | Every salon, and the new ones reps proposed. |
| Receivables | Who owes money, and for how long. |
| Cash handover | Count the cash a rep brings back. |
| Commission | Each rep's commission rate, and record a commission payment. |
| Rep performance | For each rep, this month: orders brought, delivered, waiting, commission earned, paid and still owed. Use the month buttons to look at another month. |
| Investors | Who invested, how much, and the profit you decided for them. |
| People, Team, Devices, Territories, Products | Setting things up: invite reps and salons, approve phones, areas, products and prices. |
| Settings (owner only, from Team) | The company name and city printed on invoices, the bank accounts printed on invoices, and the limits the system works with (for example the cash a rep may hold, and when the owner must approve). |

## Investors: deciding the profit for a month

The system never works out an investor's profit for you. You, the owner or manager, decide the amount and type it in. The system then keeps the totals.

1. Open **Investors**. The **This month** box shows, for everyone together: money invested, profit decided for this month, profit paid this month, and profit still owed.
2. Under it, each investor has a line. If it says **nothing decided yet for this month**, click **Decide now**.
3. On the investor's page, the **This month** box shows the same figures for that investor. Click **Decide profit for (month)**.
4. In **Decide profit**, type the amount in rupees and keep the date inside the month the profit is for. Click **Record decision**.
5. When you pay the investor, use **Record a payout** on the same page.

Good to know:

- A decision counts in the month of the date you type on it.
- If you made a mistake, use **Reverse** in the statement and give a reason. The mistake is corrected in the month it was made, not in today's month.
- "Profit still owed" is everything decided minus everything paid, for all months together, as of today.
- Investor payouts are not taken from the office cash in the system; record the cash movement as you normally do.

## Reps: what they see

Each rep sees on their phone, on the home page, their own month: orders they brought, how many are delivered or still waiting, the commission earned and paid this month, and how much is still owed to them. They cannot see another rep's figures and cannot change anything. An order counts in the month it was placed; its commission counts in the month you approve its delivery.

## When something is held or looks wrong

- **An order is on hold.** The Overview tells you why. Most often the salon has reached its credit limit or is blocked. Either release the order (if you agree to give more credit) or cancel it with a reason. Releasing and cancelling are done on the order's own page; if your menu does not offer it yet, ask the owner.
- **A number looks wrong.** Numbers are never typed into the overview pages: they come from the records. Find the record behind it (the order, the delivery, the payment or the investor entry) and correct that record the proper way: cancel, reverse or reject it with a reason. The overview updates by itself.
- **A page says it could not load.** Reload the page. If it still fails, sign out and sign in again. If it still fails, tell the owner.
- **A refusal message appears.** The system is protecting a rule (for example, a rep's own delivery cannot be approved by the same rep). Read the message; it says what is wrong.

## A salon's page: credit, contact and payments

Open **Salons** and click a salon.

- **Credit.** Shows the plan the salon may use (cash only, up to 2 or up to 4 installments), its credit limit, what it owes plus what is on order, and what is still available. Installment orders that do not fit wait on hold.
  - The **owner** sets the plan and the limit (also to lower a limit), with a reason.
  - The owner or a manager can **Raise the limit**. A manager may raise it only by a set share and only a few times a month per salon; the page says how much, and the system refuses beyond that.
  - **Block credit** stops new installment orders; **Unblock credit** lifts it. A manager's unblocks count towards the same monthly limit. A salon whose stage is Blocked is also blocked for credit: change its stage to lift that.
  - **Credit history** lists every change, who made it and why.
- **Primary contact.** Click **Change contact** (or **Add contact**), type the name and phone, and why. The old contact is kept in the history; receipts keep the contact they were issued to.
- **Payments.** Every payment recorded for the salon, with its receipt number. A wrong payment is corrected with **Reverse** and a reason (a manager cannot reverse a payment they recorded themselves).

## Adding a salon from the office

On **Salons**, click **Add a salon**, fill in the name, type, area and, if you have them, the address and a contact phone, and click **Add salon**. A salon the office adds is approved at once. If it should also order online, invite it later on **People**.

## Changing a rep bag's limit (owner)

On **Stock**, in the Locations table, click **Change limit** next to the rep's bag, type the new limit in rupees and why, and save. Stock issues that would take the bag above its limit are refused.

## Settings (owner)

Open **Team**, then **Company settings**. Change one thing at a time: type the new value and why, then save. The page shows when each value was last changed.

- **Company details**: the name and city printed on invoices and receipts. Replace the placeholder name before printing invoices for real salons.
- **Bank accounts**: the accounts printed on invoices. Add or remove an account; every field must be filled in.
- **Limits and thresholds**: amounts are in rupees. Be careful with the cash a rep may hold: reps cannot collect more cash than this before a handover.
- Installment plans, credit rules and product groups are not changed on this page.

Only the owner can save settings; a manager who opens the page is told so.

## Reps: what they see when they are stuck

- **A day left open.** If a rep did not check out, the next morning their day page says the earlier day is still open and that the office must close it. Close it from **Rep days left open** on the Overview.
- **A phone not approved or an account suspended.** Every page tells the rep why it shows nothing, instead of an empty list.
- **Their own payments.** On a salon's page and on Cash handover, the rep sees the payments they recorded, with receipt numbers, and whether the office reversed one.
- **Sign out.** On Today and on My account.

## Salons: what they see

- An order on hold says what it waits for (the office to approve the price, or to check the payment terms), never a credit figure.
- A cancelled order says it was cancelled by the office; the salon asks the office why.
- A suspended or closed salon account is told so when it signs in.
